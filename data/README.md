# Flower Photos

390 photos of three kinds of flowers, `rose`, `sunflower` and `tulip`: 100 per class in `train/`, 20 per class in `validation/` and 10 per class in `test/`. The photos are in colour and have different sizes, from 159 to 640 pixels wide; most are 320 × 240 or 500 pixels wide.

Source: the [TensorFlow flower photos](https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/tf_flowers) collection, taken from Flickr. Every photo is licensed under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/); its photographer is listed in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).
